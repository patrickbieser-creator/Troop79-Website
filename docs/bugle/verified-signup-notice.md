## Signing up for events now needs a parent's sign-in

Starting this week, the troop password alone won't sign your family up for an event anymore.
It still gets you onto the event page — that part hasn't changed. But to actually say yes,
cancel, or claim a job, a parent now needs to sign in as *themselves*.

Why: so the troop knows who actually said yes, and so nobody but you can sign your family up
or cancel on you. It's a small change with a real payoff.

**Do this once, this week, before anything's due:**

1. Go to any event page and click Sign Up (or click Members in the top menu).
2. Click "Sign in," find your name, and we'll email you a one-time code — about a minute.
3. Enter the code and you're in. Your household is already picked for you.

That sign-in lasts you about four months on that device — this is a once-a-season thing, not
a every-time thing. The site will also offer to let you sign in next time with your phone,
fingerprint, or face instead of a code. Take it — it's the same thing your bank already uses,
and it's optional.

While you're in, open **My Profile** and make sure both parents' email addresses are on file.
That's the address we'll use.
