What Was Accomplished

The outcome: scouts can now write, save and share their own recipes, credited “Recipe by Sam K.”, add ingredients the price book doesn’t have yet, list the gear a recipe needs, and turn their own version of a troop recipe into a new one. Leaders re-credit or retire scout recipes and match new ingredients into the price book.

Releases

  • v1.138.0 — tabs and a recipe library: the Menu Monster page has Meal Planner, Recipe Library, Ingredients and Recipe Builder tabs; each day’s “Search recipes” opens a library popup with breakfast / lunch / dinner / snack filters that adds a recipe in one click.
  • v1.139.0 — scout recipes: the approved recipe editor; private drafts usable in the scout’s own menus; Share with the troop with a frozen credit; an admin Scout recipes tab to re-credit, retire and restore.
  • v1.140.0 — new ingredients, gear, my version: typed-in ingredients (private until shared, then matched or kept by a leader, with menus following the match); Gear you’ll need on recipes and on a menu’s Shopping tab and printout; Share this version as a new recipe from a meal page.
Caught on the way: retiring a recipe would have silently dropped it from every menu (the catalog was published-only); browser checks found a constant exported from a client file that broke the New recipe link and a focus bug that turned spaces into empty steps; qa-lead found a possible save/match deadlock, orphaned typed-ins holding the cap, and a unit-mixing match — all fixed.
