What Was Accomplished

The outcome: scouts can share menus with the troop and copy each other’s, parents can see their scouts’ menus, leaders can leave a note or hide a menu, scouts can add what the price book lacks, and every meal now opens right on the menu page with one Save.

Releases

  • v1.141.0 — share menus: Share tab, “Shared with the troop” on the hub and its own page, “Menus for this outing” on event pages, Copy to My menus, parent read-only view, leader Review tab (note + Hide), leader filters and a roster link. One access rule and one redaction point keep unshared drafts, prices paid and notes private.
  • v1.142.0 — release C: a new ingredient from a meal (the same typed-in as recipes, revealed when the menu is shared), “Add a package you bought” on Shopping (live in band against troop packages, else waiting), leaders’ “Packages waiting”.
  • v1.143.0 — one planning page: meals open inline on the Plan tab with one Save and one Total / Per person switch; tighter ingredient rows; “Food & Recipes” and no noun inside the planner; the old one-meal planner deleted with its diet warning ported to the meal.
Caught on the way: a revoked parent could have kept reading menus until the cookie expired (epoch check added); a scout’s “same package” probe could leak another scout’s held package, and scout packages could have ratcheted the price basis down (both fixed); the planner’s diet warning had no home in the new screens (ported before deleting).
