What Was Accomplished

The outcome: a patrol plans a menu, picks brands, sees its gear, and the troop gets one shopping list for the whole outing; afterwards any scout on the outing records what was bought. Parents and leaders can now write recipes and save menus too, and the leader tools open on what needs attention.

Releases

  • v1.153.0–v1.153.1: a single food is added in one form; signed-in people can add an ingredient (a scout’s waits for a leader); stores list updated; the recipe editor lists what needs fixing.
  • v1.154.0: People dialer on the meal’s line; A–Z add list.
  • v1.155.0: Gear tab per menu, the troop’s gear list, gear and steps on the troop’s 29 foods, store-room staples.
  • v1.156.0: brands — several per ingredient, a typed brand joins the troop’s list at once.
  • v1.157.0: What we bought — a prefilled checklist any scout on the outing can record.
  • v1.158.0: a menu’s patrol, and one merged, printable shopping list for the outing.
  • v1.159.0: the leader tools open on Needs attention; a Purchases tab.
  • v1.160.0: anyone signed in can write a recipe.
  • v1.161.0: a recipe can suggest a brand; a menu that adds it starts with that brand.
  • v1.162.0: saved menus for parents and leaders; a short form for a single food; Done on the brand chooser.
  • v1.163.0: Food & recipes as one A–Z list with tabs, search and a meal filter; problems outlined in red where they are.
Caught on the way: one commit went out under the previous release’s message (explained by a follow-up commit, history not rewritten); a store change pushed without the full suite broke a test (fixed the same hour); and the single-food short form shipped in v1.162.0 never opened for the existing foods, because they all carry steps and gear — corrected in v1.163.0. Most screens were verified by automated tests rather than by eye.
