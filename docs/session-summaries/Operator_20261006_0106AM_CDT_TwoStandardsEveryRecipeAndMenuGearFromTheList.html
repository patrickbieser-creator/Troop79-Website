What Was Accomplished

The outcome: a leader sees every food, recipe and menu in one wide list each, with the controls on the row; a save the form can’t take says why beside the button and marks the field; the recipe editor has one bar and one dark button; a food keeps “vegetarians get veggie bacon instead” as a note on itself; gear is chosen, never typed; and the High Cliff menu can be given to the patrol that planned it.

Releases

  • v1.176.0: Food & recipes is a screen-wide table (kind, meals, each person gets, variations, cost per person, status). A single food opens under its row; a recipe opens its own page; the filters travel with you and come back.
  • v1.177.0: Scout recipes shows every scout recipe with owner, dates, sharing, who can edit, and Edit / Copy / Rename / Change credit / Retire — leaders may now edit a scout’s recipe. A Menus tab with the same shape. Price changes get Edit and Acknowledge. Close on the recipe page; the Save row pinned to the bottom with the blocking reason in words.
  • v1.178.0 / .1: the two standards on the recipe editor: Save stays clickable and a click on an incomplete form marks the field, switches to its tab, focuses it and says “Can’t save yet: …”; one sticky bar with Save, Discard, Publish and More actions…; quiet “+ Add” links; a segmented control for a diet’s state; a titled box per variation. Previous / Next and the scout record’s Menus button removed.
  • v1.179.0: a single food keeps its diet swaps; each flagged diet is answered right under the row (Same as everyone / Instead… / Not suitable); only a second ingredient makes a recipe; “Make it a single food” says what it drops first.
  • v1.180.0 / v1.181.0: gear is picked from the master list everywhere (nothing added on the fly; anything off the list is dropped and named); Merge into… on the Gear tab; the inline short form in its own box.
  • v1.182.0: each meal carries its own gear (wash basins, soap), rolled up with recipe and menu gear on the Gear tab and the printed sheet; Change owner and Set patrol on the Menus tab, with a patrol credit line; PayPal as a payment method; “Troop trailer” is “4th Floor Northwoods”.

Design work

  • Blocked saves: Jenna inventoried 21 gated controls (six behaviours) and wrote the rule now in AGENTS.md: greyed means nothing to do, never not valid yet.
  • Control hierarchy: Jenna counted four primaries on one screen and wrote the one-bar rule, also in AGENTS.md. Patrick agreed to both as written and to one exception (target pickers stay greyed until chosen).
  • Single food vs variations: Jenna’s walk-through found the round trip destroyed data; the model changed to “one food, one line; diet answers are notes on it.”
  • Gear: tech-lead’s design (no id switch, server-side resolve, meal gear in the plan JSON) was built as two releases by build agents from his brief and gated here.
  • The scout planner: Jenna’s MACRO pass (travel-booking patterns applied to a campout) is parked in Plans/Menu-Monster-Planner-Flow.md with an audit, a stepped flow, eight guidelines and five decisions for Patrick.
Verification honesty: every release passed lint, typecheck, the full suite and the build before it shipped. Browser eyes-on was local dev only: the Food & recipes list, a food under its row, a recipe page, the new control bar and a refused save. The gear releases and the later batch were built by sonnet agents and gated, not reviewed independently. A production audit before the gear change found no stored gear name off the master list. Patrick’s eyes-on list is in BACKLOG.
