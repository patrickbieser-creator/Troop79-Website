What Was Accomplished

The outcome: a scout at a meal can type a food the troop has never heard of and have it on the meal in four fields; say that the gluten-free bread is only for the two gluten-free scouts and see it priced that way; move through Who’s eating, Meals, Gear and Shopping as steps; and a leader can find hot cocoa, keep a scout’s Hot chocolate for the troop, and star the brand to suggest — all from one list.

Releases

  • v1.186.0: Who’s eating is its own step and screen; a meal’s recipe shows Steps and Gear as two lists, with gear descriptions.
