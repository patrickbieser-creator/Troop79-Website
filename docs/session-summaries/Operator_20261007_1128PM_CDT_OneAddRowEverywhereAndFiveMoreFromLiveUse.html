What Was Accomplished

The outcome: every list in Menu Monster now ends the same way — one quiet link, the search on tap, Cancel in sight — on the scout side and the leader side; every quantity is a plain box; a brand gets its size, price and store the moment it is typed; a menu records who planned it; the planner runs the full width of a desktop. Patrick, after seeing Phase 1 live: “UX is better.”

Leg times, hardened (v1.198.2)

qa-lead’s three warnings on v1.191.0 closed: a family’s arrival or departure time is checked before the signup RPC and before any write — a real time within two days of the event, dropped when drivers aren’t needed — and the ride board refuses a rider who isn’t on the signup instead of skipping the wave check.

The add pattern (v1.199.0, v1.200.0)

Jenna inventoried ten places the dashed add row appeared and recommended a bordered sub-card plus one grouped add row; Brad built a prototype at 390px and 720px with an A/B toggle. Patrick picked B (links at rest, search on tap), asked for a visible Cancel, no hints, plain number boxes instead of dialers, “Choose brand(s)”, and no + Ingredient on a single food. Two phases shipped the standard to every site, with admin twins on admin tokens so the two colour systems stay apart. Along the way the database tests’ cleanup was found sweeping every x- ingredient — the id shape of a real scout typed-in that the nightly sync had just mirrored in — and was rescoped to the rows a test creates.

Brand detail and Planned by (v1.201.0, v1.202.0–v1.202.2)

“Sour Cream & Onion 4 Chips” was a placeholder noun on a brand with no package. Brad extended the prototype; Patrick chose the dialog, with Store a pull-down from the approved stores list, now enforced server-side. Planned by — the scouts who planned a menu, picked from the active roster, informational only — got a join table on production and a place at the foot of Who’s eating after a two-column detour Patrick rejected. The diet counts now sit under People with one “of whom”.

The meal line and one Gear list (v1.203.0)

A meal’s People box sits on the meal’s own line; the old line reads “Food and recipes”; gear is one list, the foods’ gear first and anything added marked, every item countable and removable through a per-meal override the Gear step honours; the shopping summary moved up beside the menu’s name and the planner lost its right column.

Verification

Every release passed lint, typecheck, the full suite and the build; qa-lead reviewed each (one FAIL on a keyboard defect, fixed before commit; the rest PASS-WITH-WARNINGS with the real ones fixed). Nothing in the app was browser-checked this session; Brad verified his prototypes in headless Chrome. Patrick’s eyes-on list is in the backlog.
