What Was Accomplished

The outcome: leaving the card page no longer looks like a lost order. The treasurer can see the sale, and who needs a nudge, on one tab, and leaders can be copied on every order.

Leaving Stripe without paying (v1.226.0)

  • Back from Stripe lands on the order page, which says the card payment wasn't finished and offers card, Venmo or PayPal.
  • Customers can cancel their own unpaid online order before the round's cutoff.
  • Admin and the scout page mark “Card not finished”.

Sales Dashboard (v1.227.0)

A new tab under Wreath Sale shows:

  • Sales, collected, estimated profit and how many scouts are selling.
  • A “Needs a hand” list linking to the filtered Orders tab.
  • Round deadlines and sales by day.
  • Sellers with goal progress, and best sellers.
  • How customers paid, where orders came from, and how wreaths get there.
  • Gifts, delivery and card fees.

Order copies (v1.228.0–v1.228.1)

  • Up to five addresses in Wreath Settings, using the same checks as the event sign-up leader list.
  • Each copy is its own email, with “Copy:” in the subject and a line naming who the original went to.
  • After Patrick's review, the address boxes were styled to match the other fields and widened.
