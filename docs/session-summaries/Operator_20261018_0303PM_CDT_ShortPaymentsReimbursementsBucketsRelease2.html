Wreath orders paid short

  • Mark paid has an Amount received box, filled in with the total. The ledger books what came in, and the order keeps its total.
  • The order reads “Accepted $X short”. The Dashboard's Collected tile and the scout's Paid total count real money. The troop absorbs the shortfall, so the scout's credit is unchanged.

Reimbursements

  • Mark paid is a dialog. A matching unlinked ledger payout is offered and preselected, and linking it closes the request with no second row. Recording a new payout is still the alternative.
