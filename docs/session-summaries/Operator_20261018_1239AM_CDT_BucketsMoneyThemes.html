What Was Accomplished

The outcome: High Cliff's Money tab and snapshot now show its $516.93 of expenses, which were invisible before. All 39 cleaned-up labels are now Buckets, and the five 2026 events with money are each tied to theirs.

Event Money tab (v1.228.2, v1.229.0)

  • Record payment goes away once a person is paid in full or owes nothing, matching the Roster tab.
  • The tiles now read Income, Expenses, Still due, Net, Total expected payments (formerly “Owed”).
  • Who owes what shows how each person paid and when.

Financial Ledger (v1.230.0–v1.232.0)

  • A Method column answers “how did this money arrive?”.
  • A one-time update linked 93 production rows to their events: Summer Camp ’26 and ’27, High Adventure, Rummage Sale.
  • “Activity” became “Bucket” on every finance screen.

Buckets (v1.233.0–v1.235.0)

  • Every ledger row and calendar event points at one bucket. The migration was rehearsed on production and rolled back first; the real run gave 39 buckets, 0 gaps and 459 rows.
  • Record, Edit and the bulk bar pick from the list. Making a new bucket takes two confirmations, with the closest existing buckets offered first.
  • An event's bucket can be set on its Money tab or its calendar Entry tab.
  • The Bucket Report gained a Buckets tab: every bucket with its events and use, plus rename, merge, and delete for unused buckets.
  • tech-lead reviewed the plan; qa-lead passed the build, and four of its warnings were fixed before production.
