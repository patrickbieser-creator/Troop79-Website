What Was Accomplished

The outcome: people Patrick chooses can open “Meeting Planner” from the menu under their name and see Sunday’s whole board. With P2, the SPL and leaders can run it: answer for someone, assign a teacher, add an offer, and (adults only) send requests and nudges.

Design review and plan

  • Reviewed the meeting-plan-II handoff against v1 and settled the open questions with Patrick. Everything is in Plans/Meeting-Planner-II.md as Decisions 1–13, with 10a–10c added during the build.
  • Brad built phone mock-ups of the board, the drawer and Meeting Live, then added “Add an offer” to every view.
  • Merit badge nights become ordinary meetings built from focus sets. “Session” is renamed “rotation”, and a class can repeat across rotations.

P1, live (v1.240.0)

  • /planner shows the This Sunday board read-only: attendance bars, ready to teach, requests with everyone qualified grouped by their answer, offers with likely fits, who hasn’t answered, and the Everyone wall. It refreshes itself and works on a phone.
  • Three new rights, granted one person at a time: View, Use, and Seed. Planner rights are not leader rights: tech-lead found that any right used to count as “leader” in Admin, the Library and Menu Monster, and that is fixed.

P2, built on a branch (v1.241.0)

  • Every name opens a drawer: a person’s answer, a scout’s picks, or an adult’s teaching. Changes save on tap and record who entered them. There is also an “Enter for someone” search.
  • Assign, Add an offer (with a teacher), Send request and Nudge families. The emails are for adults only, and the SPL can edit but never sends.
  • No teaching cap for adults: a migration re-creates two database functions without the limit of 2.
  • tech-lead reviewed each phase’s approach and qa-lead reviewed each build. Every must-fix is done, and the warnings were fixed with tests.

Decisions

IDDecision
D-416The Meeting Planner is a public page at /planner with three rights (view, edit, seed) granted one person at a time. Planner rights are never leader rights.
D-417A viewer sees everything. Editors, including the SPL, change the board. Email is adults only.
D-418No teaching cap for adults. The matcher’s limits are suggestions only.
D-419Rotations, not sessions: open-ended, with an optional location. A class can repeat across rotations. Retakes are allowed and marked with a star. No patrol grouping. Meeting Live’s saved placement replaces the matcher.
D-420Merit badge nights are ordinary meetings built from focus sets. The stations and rotations screens are retired in P5.
D-421A visitor on Meeting Live becomes a guest person. Editors record attendance. A red banner appears when the viewer can’t record it.
D-422An offer names a teacher until P3 seeding. P2 splits into P2a and P2b.
D-423Planner phases are built in a git worktree on a feature branch and ship on Patrick’s word, migration first.
