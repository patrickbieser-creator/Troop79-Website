What Was Accomplished

The outcome: a leader can now go from “nobody is teaching First Aid” to an email in the right inboxes in a few clicks. People answer with one tap, and the board fills in as they do.

Invites by email (T-5)

  • “Invite teachers…” on a topic nobody is teaching emails the people worth asking. A tap on Yes puts them on the board as the teacher.
  • “Offer to scouts…” and “Offer a requirement to scouts…” email each scout’s parents (always) and the scout from 13. Yes makes it the scout’s first pick and marks them coming.
  • A link answers only its own invite and never signs anyone in. It works once and closes at 6:00 PM on the meeting day. qa-lead reviewed both phases twice, and every required fix is in. Both phases were checked end to end in the browser.

Teaching blocks, times and parts

  • A block is one adult teaching one group: up to 12 per meeting, numbered first half then second. The halves are a guide, never a limit.
  • Every requirement has an expected teaching time, 15 minutes to start, edited in Req codes. A requirement with parts takes their total.
  • First Aid, lashings and fitness list their parts wherever they appear on the board.

A calmer Sunday board

  • “Wanted, no teacher” shows who to ask, not every adult who could teach it.
  • The ten counts sit on one line, tinted for scouts and adults, and the block-by-block match moved to Group Sessions.
  • Meeting Plan is its own entry on the admin top bar.
