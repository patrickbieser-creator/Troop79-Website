What Was Accomplished

The outcome: the planning team can now seed a meeting from focus sets and then run it live on their phones. That covers rotations, repeated classes, moving scouts, attendance into Roll Call, and visitors. The pick lists also follow the rank rules: a Scoutmaster conference appears only at 95% of the rank, and a board of review appears once the rest of the rank is done.

The fix (v1.241.1)

  • Every rank’s Scoutmaster conference now waits until 95% of the rank is done. Scout, Star, Life and Eagle had been missing from the rule.
  • An Assign is tied to the picks it was made for. When the last of those picks is removed, the assignment goes too, unless the teacher signed up on their own. Mindy’s orphaned row in production was deleted.

P3: focus sets and seeding (v1.242.0)

  • Focus sets: named groups of requirements, each one a single pick. A meeting can be seeded from a set or with single rank and merit badge requirements, using a server-side search. Seeding needs the Seed right.
  • A board of review can be picked once every other requirement of the rank is done, and an open Scoutmaster conference doesn’t block it.
  • qa-lead failed the first build because some sessions showed raw keys. That was fixed and re-checked.

P4: Meeting Live (v1.243.0)

  • Once anyone edits the meeting, the plan is saved and replaces the automatic match everywhere it shows.
  • Rotations are open-ended and classes can repeat across them. Conflicts show as lines. Scouts move by drag or “Move to”. Two leaders can edit at once without overwriting each other.
  • Attendance taken on Live goes to the meeting’s Roll Call, with ledger credit, on the meeting day. “+ Add as visitor” records a guest.
  • Each part had a tech-lead design review and a qa-lead build review. Every must-fix and the important warnings were fixed with tests.

Menu Monster test flake

  • “Swap for…” focuses the search one animation frame later. On a busy machine the test’s Escape arrived before that focus. The test now waits for the focus first. A forced 300 ms delay reproduced the exact failure.
