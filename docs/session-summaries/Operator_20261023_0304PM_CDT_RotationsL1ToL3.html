What Was Accomplished

The outcome: a planner no longer has to press Start or chase drift. Once a meeting is published and has a plan, its rotations exist, late sign-ups land in a class marked New, scouts who say they can’t come leave, and every offering has a class with one teacher. Hand moves are respected. Each phase passed the full suite and a qa-lead review before shipping.

Rotations L1: keeps up with sign-ups

  • Late picks drop into a class with room and a free rotation, tagged New until someone marks them seen.
  • A scout moved by hand stays put; one who says Can’t or drops the pick leaves unless they were placed by hand.

Rotations L2: every offering has a class, and one teacher

  • Anything on the plan gets a class automatically; removing the last class sets that offering aside.
  • The class card picks the lead teacher; a teacher who said they can’t come can’t be chosen. A repeat can keep its own teacher.

Rotations L3: Meeting Live is now Rotations

  • The tab and address are Rotations; old links still work. Start and Discard are gone: More actions offers Suggest an arrangement and Reset (Reset is refused after the meeting).
  • Build a Plan shows Rotation 1·2·3 on each row. Families see only classes that have a teacher.

Also

  • Admin’s Has/Needs retired; its links open the planner tab.
  • Planner screens sit 28px from the edge (16px on a phone), checked at phone and iPad widths.
  • Build a Plan’s buttons read Plans · Suggestions · Requirements · Focus sets · A mix.
