What Was Accomplished

The outcome: the planner’s Suggestions no longer offer requirements that need a lake, a campout or a visit to an official. Leaders tag requirements on Lookups & Admin with any number of classes, and anything that doesn’t fit a meeting is listed under “Needs another setting”. Nothing a leader adds by hand is filtered.

Requirement classes (v1.256.0)

  • Eight classes: Troop meeting, Campout, Outdoor event, Aquatics, Service project, Home / independent, Expert / external visit, Leadership / administrative. They can be added, edited and retired; one in use can’t be deleted.
  • Requirements that needed an outing were tagged to match, and tenure, Scout spirit, positions of responsibility, the Eagle project and badge counts were tagged Leadership, so suggestions didn’t change on the day of the switch.
  • Internal Requirement Codes shows each requirement’s tags, filters by class, and ticks them in two columns, A to Z.

Lookups & Admin landing page (v1.256.0)

  • Five groups of links instead of one long page; each opens its card on its own, expanded, with a Back link.
  • Links from the admin home, a merit badge page and the planner go straight to their card.

Wreath card payments (v1.255.1)

  • A customer who chooses card is told so, with no Venmo or PayPal lines; a few had paid twice.
  • Once Stripe confirms, the troop sends “Paid by credit card”, once, with copies to the order-copy addresses.

Also

  • Adult Skills and Scout Instructors list only people who teach, with Add and Delete (v1.255.0).
  • Gene Brown’s sign-off initials (GB) added in production; a plan for editing initials on the person record is ready.
  • A Troop 79 design system published on claude.ai for Claude Design work; the nightly backup moved to noon.
