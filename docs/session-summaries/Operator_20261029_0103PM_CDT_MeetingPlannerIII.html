What Was Accomplished

The outcome: no scout is forgotten. Pick the night’s focus sets and every active scout is placed in a group for the rank they’re working on. Older scouts take one of the night’s merit badges or teach. Any scout with nothing to do shows as a tinted row, ready to place by hand.

This week (P0–P7)

  • Group cards with requirement counts, RSVP-tinted names and teacher slots. A requirement with sub-requirements opens on a click.
  • A Merit badge group with a Teach / badge switch. A night can offer several badges, or badges only.
  • Coverage line, Place… / Change / Let placement decide, Live at the meeting and the Theme calendar for eight weeks.
  • Publish snapshot: one email per family with the options and suggestions, and a link to answer without signing in, ticking two or three things. The member meeting page shows the same.
  • Who’s coming, Build a Plan, Invite and Rotations retired, along with their tables.

From Patrick’s review

  • The checklist reads white = still to do and green = done, with a stronger green to meet contrast rules. Rank sub-requirements (4a.1–4a.8) get their own boxes, and a part-done merit badge requirement is half green.
  • Scouts who already earned a badge are no longer put in its group (the Veronica K / First Aid bug).
  • A group can have several teachers, and the planner’s menus open in view instead of being cut off at the bottom of the screen.

Next screen: Emails

  • Patrick added an Emails screen to the design: invites, reminders and a sent log with scheduling and open tracking. Eleven questions with recommendations are waiting in Plans/Meeting-Planner-III-Emails.md.
