What Was Accomplished

The outcome: a leader can see who got which email about a meeting, families confirm or change a plan in one tap, and the planner suggests who is ready for a review and who should sit it.

Families

  • Each scout’s card shows the plan, ticked; one answer covers coming and the plan. Unticking applies at once; additions go to the planners as suggestions.
  • Cards are headed with the scout’s full name and plan on the member page, and every card has “Clear this answer”.
  • The teaching section is a card of its own, with “Scouts are asking for these” as a separate card.

Emails

  • The Emails tab sends plan emails, teacher invites and reminders now, one at a time, each logged, with a Sent log and Send again. Rebuilt to match the Claude Design prototype.
  • Publish snapshot, Remind, Send request and Ask by email are retired, and their storage dropped.

Planner

  • The scout drawer shows where they are tonight, with Move, Help teach and Unassign.
  • Suggestions returns, leading with scouts ready for a Scoutmaster conference or board of review and who should sit each.
  • Has/Needs: a badge’s first click shows who earned it. Find requirements: rank pills and an aligned In sets column. This week: date-only meeting buttons; attendance prompt can be dismissed.
  • Saved meeting plans (save the criteria, load to replace) built and reviewed on a branch; not shipped.
