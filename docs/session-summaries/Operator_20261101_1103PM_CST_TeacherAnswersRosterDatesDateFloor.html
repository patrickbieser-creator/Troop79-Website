What Was Accomplished

The outcome: every teacher answer now lands in one place, whether it comes from the email, the family page, or a text to Patrick, and the Sent log shows at a glance who has said yes.

Teacher emails and answers

  • The “can you teach?” email opens the teacher’s family card; the old teach pages are gone. An adult asked about a group they aren’t on answers under “We asked you to teach”.
  • A scout teacher is emailed at their own address with their parents copied (Youth Protection).
  • A yes holds through later emails; only a plan change asks again.
  • In an adult’s drawer, “Will they teach?” records They said yes / They said no for them, marked “entered by PB”.

Planner screens

  • Responses this week card under Adults; a one-line-per-email Sent log with answer pills, filters, paging and a 30-second refresh; open tracking through links.troop-79.com.
  • Family-page saves show Saving… then ✓ Saved.

Roster and dates

  • Sortable Created on every roster tab, and Created + Left on Inactive Scouts, derived from the ledger.
  • No year before 1900: every date field says so, and the database refuses it on all 226 date and timestamp columns.
  • Production typos fixed by hand: Oliver Vest’s 8 Communication MB entries (year 0025 → 2025) and Jenna Scarpace’s birthdate (0991 → 1991).
