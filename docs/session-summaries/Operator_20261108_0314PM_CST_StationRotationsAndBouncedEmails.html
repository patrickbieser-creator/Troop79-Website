What Was Accomplished

The outcome: the first Climbing merit badge night (meeting 141) runs as three 20-minute stations. Every scout without the badge is drafted into Group 1, 2 or 3 and rotates through all of them. Families get each scout's route in the plan email.

Station rotations

  • Groups · Stations switch on This week; a time goal on each focus set; station order, a rotation grid (stacked on a phone), and per-group checklists.
  • The draft groups scouts by the rank they're working on and merges from the top. Scout and Tenderfoot stay whole. Teachers stay at one station all evening.
  • Tap a scout to move them by group number or send them Back to the draft. Also tonight puts a scout in a second, optional place, such as a conference or Citizenship in the Community. Double assignments are called out, never blocked.
  • Station teachers get emails and teacher cards. Live at the meeting has a Station teachers card and a route card per group. Saved plans remember Stations.
  • Brad prototyped the screens first; a printable per-group sign-off sheet for tonight came from a script.
  • Bug fixed: the focus card was empty while Stations was on.

Email deliverability

  • Outlook/Hotmail bounces: troop-79.com had no DMARC record. Patrick added it (p=none), verified live.
  • The Sent log shows the receiving server's bounce reason; a bounced email has Send again.

Housekeeping

  • The nightly local mirror sync was failing on a production test invite with no token. Deleted the invite and validated the constraint; the sync is clean (123 tables).
  • Production topic titles for the three Climbing stations updated to match the focus sets.
