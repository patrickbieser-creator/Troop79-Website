What Was Accomplished

The outcome: a leader can open last week's plan exactly as it stood that night, print any plan as a checklist to tick off as sign-offs are recorded, and find everything about a merit badge in one place in the Library.

Meeting Planner: past meetings and the plan PDF

  • "← Earlier weeks" opens past meetings, view only, with each scout's rank and progress as of that night, plus a "What happened" card: roll call, sign-offs dated that day, ranks and badges earned.
  • "Print PDF" on any meeting, past or coming: groups and teachers, a box per planned requirement, station grids, conferences and boards, who can't come. Always as of the start of the meeting.
  • qa-lead review: two reads that could stop at 1000 rows fixed, closed meetings refused, plus small hardening.

People and money

  • Merging a duplicate or promoting a Webelos guest now moves payments, scout account credits, reimbursements and wreath records to the surviving person.
  • Maya Sankpal-Tatera's $108.81 scout account, stranded by an earlier merge, is back on her record.
  • Three Webelos parents were added as members; no new classification was needed.

Merit badges

  • Fast Entry: opening a badge from a search no longer hides requirements that don't match the search.
  • Admin MB Progress retired. Its two extras moved to the Library: "+N working" on the badge list and the counselors on each badge page (signed-in only). Old links redirect.
  • Signed-in members see full names on the Advancement roster, the Clipboard and the badge grids; visitors still see "First L.".
