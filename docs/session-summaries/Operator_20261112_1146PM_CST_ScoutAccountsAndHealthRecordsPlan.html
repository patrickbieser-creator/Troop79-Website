What Was Accomplished

The outcome: leaders can see every scout account in one list, open any account’s full history, and move a departed scout’s balance into the troop’s general funds in one confirmed step. The health records feature has every decision answered and clickable prototypes; building starts tomorrow.

Money: Scout Accounts (live)

  • Every scout account — including scouts who have left, aged-out scouts who are now adults, Webelos and the Scholarship Fund — with its balance and last activity. Filter by All, Active, Inactive or Adults, or search by name. The total ties to the Financial Ledger.
  • Clicking a name shows that account’s history beside the list: date, what it was for, the amount, and the balance after each entry, with who recorded it.
  • Move to troop fund… takes a departed scout’s whole balance into the troop’s available funds, one scout at a time. Voiding it in the Financial Ledger undoes it. Two people moving the same balance at once still records it only once.

Health records (planned)

  • Files are encrypted by the site and stored in a private Bunny storage area with no public address; they are only ever shown through the site, and every view is logged.
  • Who can open them: six named leaders, each family for its own household, and any adult attending a trip for that trip’s attendees from a week before until two days after.
  • A person is complete when the BSA form and insurance card are on file (plus Part C for summer camp and high adventure) and an authorized adult has reviewed them.
  • Prototypes cover the parent upload on a phone, a leader on a campout, the event’s Medical tab with binder printing, the troop-wide list, and the access log.
