What Was Accomplished

The outcome: a family can sign up for an outing, pay by card or say how they will pay, and upload the health form and insurance card from a phone. A leader can see who is ready for a trip, open a record at the event, print the binder and email the families who are missing something.

Event sign-up: how will you pay?

  • Every sign-up with a fee lists Venmo, PayPal, check, cash, scout account and card as separate choices. Patrick confirmed a real card payment works.
  • Scout account always shows the balance; it is a request the treasurer applies. Card adds the fee (2.9% + 30¢) and books itself on the event’s Money tab.
  • Venmo, PayPal and check details are entered once under Lookups › Payment details.
  • Pressing a chosen answer (Attending) again now takes it back.

Health records

  • Parents add the BSA form, insurance card and immunization record under Members › Health forms. Each file is encrypted before it is stored. Patrick confirmed uploads work on the live site.
  • Leaders use /health: the trip list with each person’s standing, one person’s record with who to call, the Reviewed tick, the named list and the access log.
  • Named leaders see everyone. Any adult attending a trip sees that trip’s people from 7 days before to 2 days after. Scouts never.
  • Print binder builds one PDF per trip; Remind families emails each family its own list; outing sign-ups show a note for missing forms.

Housekeeping

  • Next.js went from 16.2.6 to 16.4.6, closing the open security advisories.
  • The local database project was renamed from next-app to Troop-79 in Docker.
Not yet proven: the binder was tested with generated images and PDFs, never with real phone scans or a filled BSA form, and the leader pages were not checked signed in on the live site.
