What Was Accomplished

The outcome: a leader can see every proof approval from the past two months and what it wrote to the ledger. The audit page finds scouts whose logged service hours already meet a rank requirement. Health-records leaders who are admins have a Health Forms list showing every person's documents and status, with each record opening beside the list.

Advancement › Approvals (v1.271.0)

  • A new tab after Submit & Present lists approved proof submissions from the past two months, newest first.
  • Each opens a view-only record: who filed it, the write-up and photos, who approved it, and the ledger entry it created, including whether it was later deleted.

Audits › Service Hours (v1.272.0)

  • Tenderfoot 7b, Second Class 8e and First Class 9d count all service hours logged; Star 4 and Life 4 count hours since the previous rank.
  • The sign-off date defaults to the day the hours were reached; Life 4 reminds the leader to check the conservation hours.

Admin › Health Forms (v1.273.0, phases 1–2)

  • Brad designed the screen, Jenna reviewed it, and Patrick settled who gets in, who is listed and what /health keeps.
  • People & Setup now reads Roster · Health Forms · Access. Only admins on the health viewers list see it.
  • The list has a mark per document and a status, status tabs with totals, a Type switch, search and sortable columns. A record opens in a side panel with Prev and Next; opening is logged first.
  • Who can open health records and the Access log moved into the admin. qa-lead passed it with two fixes, both applied.
