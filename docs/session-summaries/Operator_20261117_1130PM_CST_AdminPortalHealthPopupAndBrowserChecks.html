What Was Accomplished

The outcome: a leader finds any admin screen, task or report from the rail, an A–Z or Ctrl K, and sees what needs them on Home. A health viewer can review, fix and chase a family's health forms from one pop-up, and every change is logged first.

Leader workspace (Admin Portal phases 1–6)

  • One registry lists every admin screen, report, task and setup page; a test fails any page left out.
  • A rail with area menus (Do · Screens · Reports · Setup), pins that follow you between devices, and Everything A–Z.
  • Home is a dashboard: Needs you today, This week, your shortcuts, At a glance tiles, and the old Advancement dashboard cards. New Missing BSA IDs report.
  • Find anything (Ctrl K or /) searches screens and tasks, plus people, events and articles; never health or money.
  • Utilities and the old section bar are retired; Financial Ledger tasks open from a link.

Health record pop-up

  • View opens a wide two-pane pop-up: the family's note and who to contact on the left, a card per form on the right.
  • A missing or expired form gets a red card with Email reminder and Upload for family; other cards offer View, Download and Change…
  • Each form has its own Good until date; Mark reviewed can be undone; reminders no longer need a trip.
  • Patrick's follow-ups: the immunization record reads On Part C, Attached or Missing; a missing Part C is red; the From the family box is always shown, and leaders can paste in a family's emailed note; long notes show four lines with Show more; PDFs open in the browser.

Browser checks

  • npm run shot signs in as a local leader, opens a page at desktop and phone widths in headless Chrome or Firefox, runs steps, and reports console errors.
  • Claude in Chrome stays for short production checks with Patrick at the machine; the checklist to make it reliable is in the backlog.
