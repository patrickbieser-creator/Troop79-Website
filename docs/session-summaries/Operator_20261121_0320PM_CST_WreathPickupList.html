What Was Accomplished

The outcome: on pickup day, leaders see how many of each wreath every scout takes home, and each scout gets a page listing every order credited to them, with customer phone, address and anything still unpaid.

Pickup list (Admin › Wreath Sale)

  • A totals table counts every item by where it goes (scouts, lot pickup, troop delivers, donated) to check against the Rose order.
  • One card per scout with item counts, a tick box, and each customer's order; then Customer pickup at the lot, Troop delivers (with addresses) and Donated to veterans.
  • Print keeps only the list, two cards across. Also under Money and Reports, and in Ctrl K.

Print scout pages

  • One page per scout to take home: a heading line, the scout's name, big item counts, and each order with phone, address and unpaid amount.

Fix: every credited order on the scout's sheet

  • W-1017 was credited to Jack Porter but set to troop delivery, so it was missing from his sheet. Sheets now list every order credited to the scout, with a note on any the scout isn't delivering.
