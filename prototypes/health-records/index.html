Health records

Families upload the BSA health form, insurance card and immunizations from a phone. Leaders on a trip can open them; the admin sees who is missing, reminds families, prints a binder, and controls and audits who can look.

Prototypes

  • Flow 1 · Parent phone

    The /member “Health forms” card and the event sign-up Notice as ways in; the household list; Add / Replace drawer with photo or file pages, reorder and remove, Front/Back card slots, date signed → “Good until”; view, remove with confirm. A dev bar simulates slow upload, page 2 failing, a 14 MB file and offline.

  • Flow 2 · Leader on a campout phone

    /health: search first, This trip · Everyone, status badges; one person with what the family told us first, Call / Text, documents and a full-screen viewer with Download for printing. States: missing, no health access, file won’t load, offline.

  • Flow 3a · Event Medical tab desktop

    Fall Camp’s workbench with Medical beside Money: summary line, All · Needs attention, table and person panel, Remind families (two-step), Print binder → PDF preview with cover sheet and stub pages.

  • Flow 3b–3d · Roster, Access, Access log desktop

    Troop-wide Health forms tab (worst first), the Access screen’s new Health column with a dirty-gated Save, and the read-only access log with filters.

Open questions for Patrick

  1. Who gets health access, and is it tied to being at the event?
  2. Do scouts 13 and older see their own forms? Default: no.
  3. Keep insurance member IDs as typed fields? Default: no, photo only.
  4. How long are files kept after a scout leaves? Default: erased 90 days after going inactive.
  5. Is Part C per event (treks) or per person?
  6. Can families see who viewed their records?
  7. Confirm reversing the “medical not tracked” policy.

Raised while building

  1. “Date the doctor signed” fits Part C only; Parts A and B are signed by the parent. Should the field say “Date signed”, and which date sets “Good until”?
  2. Does “complete” on the event tab mean the health record only, or also insurance and immunizations? (Jonah has no card back and no immunizations but counts as complete here.)
  3. Can a leader without health access still see “Things the family told us” (allergies) in an emergency? Here they see status and the emergency contact only.
  4. What happens to a printed binder after the trip (returned, shredded), and should the cover sheet say so?
  5. Remind families emails every adult in the household. Should scouts 13+ with a verified address get it too?
