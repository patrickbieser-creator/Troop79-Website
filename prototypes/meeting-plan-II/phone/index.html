Meeting Planner II · phone

390px mock-ups for review before P1. Desktop handoff: README.

This Sunday board

One column: the right-hand column moves under the header, the name search stays pinned while you scroll, teacher lists fold behind “N people can teach this ▾”, and the Everyone wall becomes one folding list per answer.

 Person drawer

A full-screen bottom sheet, not a side panel. The header, Coming control and Send/Done button stay put and only the list scrolls. Toggle Scout, Nudge families, Adult, Remind and Seed at the top.

 Meeting Live

One rotation at a time behind sticky tabs (Not scheduled · R1 · R2 · R3 · +) instead of side-by-side columns. Tap replaces drag: tap a scout for here/move/remove, tap a teacher to pick, and use Move to on each card. Shows rotation locations, repeats, the retake star, overlaps, the view-only banner and other leaders' changes.
Add an offer (on every view)

Editors (the SPL and adults with edit rights, never view-only) can add a class someone will teach: search one requirement, pick a focus group, or type a title, then an optional teacher. Board: “+ Add an offer” on Ready to teach and Offered, no takers. Drawer: Add an offer mode, plus “+ Offer something else” in Adult mode. Live: “+ Add a class” in every rotation and in Not scheduled.

Striped “Mock” bars are review controls, not part of the design. Sample data only: nothing is saved, and reloading resets each page.
