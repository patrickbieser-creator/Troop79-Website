Menu Monster — one add row per container

  • The open food is a bordered card: its ingredient lines and one + Ingredient row, with for Everyone ▾ at the row's right end, always.
  • No hint text anywhere. The card edge says the edit is local.
  • One add row per container at its own indent — + Food · + Gear after the foods, + Add a meal in the day tier below the card — and every ⋯ lists edit → swap → Share → Remove in that order.

Brand detail — what changed

  • Chips is open on its line: Any brand · Doritos · Sour Cream & Onion, and a brand nobody has sized or priced carries a quiet New.
  • Choosing a New brand — or typing one — opens its details in place: how much one bag holds, price, store. Save is grey until something is typed; Cancel and Esc close it and put focus back on the chip.
  • The quantity line never says "Chips". A brand without a size shows size? in the noun slot; once saved it reads 12 oz bags. Both open the same details — a badge that asks for something is a control.
  • Need · buying counts only brands with a known size; nothing known reads "buying —"; short is said.
  • Lines show from one chosen brand up (the live chooser waits for two) so the size control has a home. A second tap on a chosen chip unchooses it, as today.
