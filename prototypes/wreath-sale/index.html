Prototypes

Wreath sale

The customer path runs store → checkout → confirmation. Every total is computed from one set of sample orders, so the scout’s earnings, the Rose order and the books agree.

  • 1. General store/wreaths, credited to Troop 79. Deadline band, where the money goes, 19 items in five groups, donate to veterans, sticky cart (bottom bar on phones). Strip toggle: after the final cutoff.
  • 2. Scout link/wreaths/ben-b. Greeting, Ben’s message and goal bar. Try ?s=jonah-m (retired) or ?s=nobody: both fall back to the general store. The last scout link opened gets the credit.
  • 3. CheckoutEditable lines, gift, pickup or delivery with the 532 zip check, the relative box on scout orders, cover-the-fee, PayPal mock. Dashed prototype controls switch store/scout and PayPal/Release A, can force a declined payment, and show “Cutoff passed while open”.
  • 4. ConfirmationOrder number, what was ordered, how it arrives, payment status, who to contact.
  • 5. My wreath orders/member/wreaths for Ben’s family. Totals first, orders with paid/unpaid and relative flags, “+ Order taken in person”, printable delivery list, goal and message, members-only leaderboard.
  • 6. AdminOrders (filters; open an order for its Changes list and Reassign…), Sellers (add from roster, link, retire), Rose order by round, Books (per-scout rollup, 70/30, check row, close sale), Settings.
